About

My interests center on modern computer vision and multimodal AI. I enjoy going beyond using models: understanding their architecture, tracing tensor shapes through the network, reproducing papers, and improving real systems.

Current interests include Transformer-based vision models, dichotomous image segmentation, image/video generation and vision-language models.
