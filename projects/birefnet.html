Research and experimentation around high-resolution dichotomous image segmentation, with a focus on understanding hierarchical Transformer features, multi-scale feature interaction, progressive decoding, and fine boundary recovery.

Problem

Dichotomous Image Segmentation aims to separate a salient foreground object from its background. For high-resolution images, a good mask must capture both the global structure of the object and small, difficult boundaries such as thin structures, holes, hair-like regions, and irregular contours.

My work focuses on understanding and optimizing the complete path from Transformer encoder features to a high-resolution segmentation mask rather than treating the network as a black box.

01 · Hierarchical features

Swin Transformer produces feature maps at multiple spatial resolutions. Early stages retain more local detail, while deeper stages encode increasingly semantic and global information.

02 · Feature interaction

Features from different levels are combined so that semantic information can guide high-resolution reconstruction while local details remain available for boundary refinement.

03 · Progressive decoding

The decoder progressively increases spatial resolution instead of directly jumping from a low-resolution representation to the final mask.

04 · Boundary quality

High-resolution segmentation is especially sensitive to object boundaries. The optimization goal is therefore not only semantic correctness, but also accurate contour recovery.

What I am studying

Swin Transformer

Window attention, shifted windows, hierarchical feature extraction, patch merging, and the relationship between spatial resolution and channel dimension.

Patch Expanding

How decoder-side upsampling reconstructs spatial resolution and how it differs conceptually from ordinary interpolation-based upsampling.

Multi-scale decoding

How encoder features at different resolutions are aligned, fused, and passed through successive decoder stages.

DIS datasets

Experiments and analysis involving high-resolution dichotomous segmentation benchmarks, including DIS5K-based workflows.

Research mindset

Rather than only reproducing an architecture, I am working through the tensor shapes and operations stage by stage: what each feature map represents, how its resolution changes, where information is lost, and how the decoder reconstructs precise spatial structure.
