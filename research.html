Research, from
architecture to systems.

I study modern AI systems by tracing the actual computation: tensor shapes, feature hierarchies, conditioning paths, decoding stages and deployment constraints. The goal is to turn papers into understanding and understanding into reproducible experiments.

Core directions

Vision Transformers

ViT and Swin Transformer architectures, attention mechanisms, hierarchical representations, patch merging, patch expanding and spatial-semantic trade-offs.

High-Resolution Segmentation

Dichotomous Image Segmentation with BiRefNet, multi-scale feature interaction, progressive decoding and precise boundary reconstruction.

Generative Vision

Diffusion models for image generation, relighting, editing and controllable image/video synthesis.

Vision-Language Models

Multimodal understanding, image/video question answering, GPU inference and model serving with modern VLMs.

Research principle

I learn models from the inside out: start with the mathematical operation, follow the tensor through the implementation, connect it to the architecture diagram, and only then summarize the high-level idea.

paper → tensor flow → implementation → experiment → insight
